Bookora is a service marketplace where customers book vetted local professionals — cleaning, personal training, photography, beauty and wellness, tutoring, consulting, web development, home repair, pet and event services — in about two minutes, with no enquiry-and-wait. Time slots are generated live from each provider’s working hours minus their existing bookings and time off, so the customer picks a real slot, pays on Stripe and the appointment is confirmed the moment payment succeeds — money held until the job is done, free cancellation up to 24 hours before. Search and category pages compare providers side by side on price, rating, location and next open slot, and reviews can only be left after a completed booking. On the provider side: a public storefront with services and pricing, a calendar that prevents double bookings, booking-linked messaging, revenue analytics, and Stripe Connect payouts minus a transparent 10% commission charged only on completed bookings. Built with Next.js and React on Cloudflare Workers, styled with Tailwind CSS — fully responsive, accessible and installable as a PWA.
Technologies: Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, Stripe Connect, Cloudflare Workers
