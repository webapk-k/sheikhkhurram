ALYYO Clothing Co. is a US-based custom apparel manufacturer — cut & sew production, sample development, private label, embroidery and screen printing, and tech-pack support — and this is their WordPress build. A custom theme carries the header, footer, styling and assets, while the pages themselves are assembled in Elementor on the theme’s full-width template, with Gutenberg blocks for editorial content — so the team edits copy and imagery without touching code. It is a 1:1 WordPress port of the brand’s Next.js site, with every section, animation and colour preserved. The long-form homepage carries the whole brand story in one scroll: a video-backed hero, an About section with counters (10+ years, 8.5K+ clients, 95% satisfaction, 120+ brands launched), a services grid covering design consultation, fabric sourcing and production-ready tech packs, a three-step “From design to production” process, client testimonials, and a Book-a-Meeting section wired to phone and email. A separate Wholesale Shop page presents blanks and private-label styles with category filtering — accessories, activewear, outerwear, tops and uniforms. Sticky header with smooth scroll anchors, a floating enquiry prompt, fully responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile, and deployed on Cloudflare Workers for fast delivery.
Technologies: WordPress, Elementor, Custom Theme, Gutenberg, PHP, Cloudflare Workers
